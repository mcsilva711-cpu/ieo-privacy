Privacy Policy

Industrial Equipment Outlet Inventory Sync is a private inventory, order-management, and purchasing-analysis application used to manage an eBay seller account.

Information We Access

The application may access eBay listing, inventory, order, transaction, shipping, and account-related information made available through authorized eBay APIs.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage inventory, synchronize eBay listings and orders, track sales, analyze business performance, and assist with purchasing and resale decisions.

Storage and Security

Application credentials and authorized account data are used only for operating the application. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information and access credentials.

Sharing

Information obtained through eBay is not sold to third parties. It is used only for operation of the application and services necessary to provide its functionality.

Account Deletion

The application maintains an endpoint for eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. Applicable personal information is deleted when required by eBay's account-deletion process.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the application changes.
